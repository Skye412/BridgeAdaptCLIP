# BridgeAdaptCLIP-v1.6

BridgeAdaptCLIP-v1.6 is a single-variable morphology-balanced ranking
experiment. It preserves the v1.5 architecture and all v1.3/v1.5 loss weights,
optimizer settings, initialization, and evaluation protocol.

The only change is hard-positive sampling. Each defect image contributes two
equal ranking terms:

- 128 lowest-logit pixels from the complete binary defect mask;
- 128 lowest-logit pixels from a one-pixel GT skeleton generated by
  `skimage.morphology.skeletonize`.

If the skeleton contains fewer than 128 pixels, all skeleton pixels are used
and the remaining positions are filled by the lowest-logit non-skeleton defect
pixels. Both positive pools are ranked against the same 256 highest-logit GT
background pixels. The two ranking terms are averaged and retain v1.5's weight
of `0.01`. Normal images do not participate in ranking.
